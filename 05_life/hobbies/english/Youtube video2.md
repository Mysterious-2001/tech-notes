## 1. 中文总结

这段视频用《头脑特工队 2》中 Riley 进入青春期的片段来讲解自然英语口语。视频先展示 Riley 大脑总部突然被工人“拆除改造”，象征童年思维正在让位给青春期状态；接着讲解了一系列常见表达，比如 **What the heck? / clear it out / in charge / tear apart / no can do / all set / give me a sec / get up**。后半段聚焦 Riley 醒来后情绪失控、对父母不耐烦、觉得自己很糟糕的场景，并讲解 **You're always on me / lay off / overreact much / barely / gross / make a big deal / stick to the script / preview** 等表达。整体内容围绕青春期的混乱、父母的无奈，以及如何从电影片段中学习地道口语。

## 2. 分段英文整理版

### Part 1：Chaos at Headquarters

**整理后的英文：**

Huh? What the heck is that?

What are you doing?

Woo! Problem solved.

Okay, let’s clear it all out. It’s demo day.

Demo?

Wait. What’s happening? What’s going on? Are you the one in charge here?

Could you do me a favor and stop tearing headquarters apart?

**中文解释：**

这一段是 Riley 大脑总部突然开始混乱。警报响起，工人开始拆东西，Joy 完全不知道发生了什么。这里的 **What the heck is that?** 表示震惊和困惑；**clear it all out** 是“把这里清空”；**demo day** 在这里不是“演示日”，而是 **demolition day**，意思是“拆除日”；**in charge** 表示“负责的人”；**tear something apart** 是“把某物拆得乱七八糟”。

---

### Part 2：The Workers Have Already Started

**整理后的英文：**

No can do. Didn’t you hear? Permits just came through.

Permits for what?

Banning the place, you know, for the others.

The what others? They’re not here yet?

Hey, Maji, you got that console?

Yeah, yeah, yeah. Give me a sec.

Hey. What are you doing?

All right, she’s all set.

Set with what?

Come on, Riley. Get up. Camp time.

**中文解释：**

这一段是工人解释：许可已经批下来了，所以改造已经开始，无法停止。**No can do** 是很口语的“不行，办不到”；**permits just came through** 表示“许可刚刚批下来了”；**Give me a sec** 是 “Give me a second” 的口语缩略，意思是“等我一下”；**all set** 表示“准备好了”；**get up** 在这里是“起床”。

---

### Part 3：The Lesson Explains the Scene

**整理后的英文：**

That was pure chaos. But by the end of this lesson, you’ll understand all of it.

Today, we’ll encounter every parent’s nightmare: when their child goes through puberty.

And we’ll learn a ton of English expressions and pronunciation with this hilarious clip from Inside Out 2.

The first thing that kicks things off is the alarm going absolutely haywire.

When it all starts, Sadness immediately reacts: “What the heck is that?”

**中文解释：**

视频老师开始解释这个片段，说这是一段“纯混乱”的场景，但学完后你会完全听懂。**pure chaos** 是“完全一团乱”；**by the end of this lesson** 表示“到这节课结束时”；**go through puberty** 是“经历青春期”；**a ton of** 是“很多”；**kick things off** 表示“开始某件事”；**go haywire** 表示“失控、出故障、乱套”。

---

### Part 4：What the Heck?

**整理后的英文：**

“What the heck” is a very common casual expression in English.

It’s a more polite substitute for “what the hell.”

You use it when you’re confused, surprised, or frustrated.

It can be used in questions or exclamations.

Think of when Harry Potter first discovers he’s a wizard, and his whole life turns out to be a lie.

That is very much a “What the heck is going on?” situation.

**中文解释：**

这一段讲 **What the heck**。它是 **What the hell** 的更礼貌版本，适合在惊讶、困惑、生气但又不想太粗鲁时使用。比如你看到很离谱的情况，可以说 **What the heck is going on?**，意思接近“这到底怎么回事？”

---

### Part 5：Clearing Out and Demo Day

**整理后的英文：**

When the mysterious workers show up, Joy watches in disbelief as they start trashing headquarters.

“Let’s clear it all out. It’s demo day.”

To clear something out means to remove everything from a space and empty it completely.

You might clear out your locker at the end of the school year, or clear out your desk when you leave a job.

Here, “demo” is short for “demolition,” the act of tearing something down.

Basically, Riley’s childhood mind is being demolished to make room for her teenage self.

**中文解释：**

这一段解释 **clear out** 和 **demo day**。**clear something out** 是“把某个地方清空”，比如毕业时清空储物柜、离职时清空办公桌。这里的 **demo** 不是“展示”，而是 **demolition** 的缩写，表示“拆除”。视频用这个场景比喻 Riley 的童年大脑正在被“拆掉”，为青春期的大脑腾地方。

---

### Part 6：Who’s in Charge?

**整理后的英文：**

As Joy tries to make sense of what’s happening, she goes to talk to the boss.

“Wait, what’s happening? What’s going on? Are you the one in charge here?”

To be in charge means to be the person with authority, the one making the decisions, and the one responsible for what’s happening.

Joy tries to stop the chaos, but she can’t.

“Could you do me a favor and stop tearing headquarters apart?”

**中文解释：**

Joy 想弄清楚到底谁负责，于是问：**Are you the one in charge here?** 这里 **in charge** 就是“负责、掌管”。**make sense of what’s happening** 表示“理解正在发生的事情”。她还用了一个很自然的请求句：**Could you do me a favor and...?**，意思是“你能不能帮个忙……？”

---

### Part 7：No Can Do and All Set

**整理后的英文：**

The worker says, “No can do. Didn’t you hear? Permits just came through.”

“No can do” is a casual, slightly playful way of saying “That’s not possible.”

He isn’t being mean. He’s just letting Joy know that the wheels are already in motion, and there’s nothing to be done about it.

Then another worker says, “Give me a sec.”

A moment later, she says, “All right, she’s all set.”

“All set” means ready or prepared.

**中文解释：**

这一段讲几个很实用的表达。**No can do** 是“办不到、不行”，语气有点轻松。**The wheels are already in motion** 表示“事情已经开始推进了”。**There’s nothing to be done about it** 是“现在也没办法了”。**Give me a sec** 是“等我一下”。**All set** 是“准备好了”。

---

### Part 8：Riley Wakes Up

**整理后的英文：**

Riley, you aren’t packed yet.

You’re always on me. Can’t you just lay off for like one second?

Uh, overreact much?

I barely touched it. Those morons broke the console.

Riley, what’s wrong?

Oh, Mom looks sad.

I’m the worst.

Oh no, honey.

I barely touched it.

That’s what I said.

Let the professional handle this.

I’m too gross to go to camp, or anywhere ever again.

Oh yeah, this is totally broken.

**中文解释：**

Riley 醒来后开始表现出典型青春期的情绪波动。妈妈提醒她还没收拾东西，她立刻说 **You’re always on me**，意思是“你总是盯着我、说我”。**lay off** 是“别烦我、别管我”；**overreact much?** 是讽刺别人“小题大做”；**I barely touched it** 是“我几乎没碰它”；**I’m the worst** 是“我糟透了”；**gross** 在这里是“恶心、邋遢、不舒服”。

---

### Part 9：Mom Tries to Stay Calm

**整理后的英文：**

Well, we all knew this day would come.

Remember, we agreed not to make a big deal about this.

But she really does stink.

Oh, it’s bad.

Remain calm. Stick to the prepared script.

You are not gross, honey. You’re just changing.

Remember that kind of easy?

But if you have questions—

Oh my gosh. Mom, just go away.

Well, that’s a preview of the next ten years.

**中文解释：**

这一段进入 Riley 妈妈的大脑。妈妈们早知道青春期会来，所以提醒自己 **not to make a big deal about this**，也就是“不要小题大做”。**Stick to the prepared script** 是“按照准备好的说法来，不要乱发挥”。**Oh my gosh** 是比较礼貌的惊讶或崩溃表达。最后一句 **That’s a preview of the next ten years** 很幽默，意思是“这只是未来十年的预告片”。

## 5. 句型模板

### 句型 1：What the heck is...?

- **含义：** 这到底是什么/怎么回事？
    
- **适合场景：** 表达惊讶、困惑、不满。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** What the heck is that?
    
- **我的仿写：** What the heck is this message?
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - What the heck is going on?
        
    - What the heck are you talking about?
        
    - What the heck happened to my phone?
        

---

### 句型 2：Are you the one in charge of...?

- **含义：** 你是负责……的人吗？
    
- **适合场景：** 工作、旅行、办事、找负责人。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** Are you the one in charge here?
    
- **我的仿写：** Are you the one in charge of this event?
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Are you the one in charge of the booking?
        
    - Who’s in charge of this project?
        
    - I need to talk to the person in charge.
        

---

### 句型 3：Could you do me a favor and...?

- **含义：** 你能帮我个忙，做……吗？
    
- **适合场景：** 礼貌请求别人帮忙。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** Could you do me a favor and stop tearing headquarters apart?
    
- **我的仿写：** Could you do me a favor and send me the file?
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Could you do me a favor and check this sentence?
        
    - Could you do me a favor and take a photo for us?
        
    - Could you do me a favor and wait here for a second?
        

---

### 句型 4：I’m trying to make sense of...

- **含义：** 我正在试图搞懂……
    
- **适合场景：** 工作、学习、解释复杂情况。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** Joy tries to make sense of what’s happening.
    
- **我的仿写：** I’m trying to make sense of this report.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - I’m trying to make sense of the requirements.
        
    - I’m still trying to make sense of what he said.
        
    - It took me a while to make sense of the situation.
        

---

### 句型 5：No can do.

- **含义：** 不行，办不到。
    
- **适合场景：** 轻松、口语化拒绝别人。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** No can do.
    
- **我的仿写：** Sorry, no can do. I already have plans.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - No can do. I’m in a meeting.
        
    - I’d love to help, but no can do today.
        
    - No can do. The deadline is too tight.
        

---

### 句型 6：Give me a sec.

- **含义：** 等我一下。
    
- **适合场景：** 工作聊天、点餐、查东西、回复消息。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** Give me a sec.
    
- **我的仿写：** Give me a sec. I’ll look it up.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Give me a sec. I’m almost ready.
        
    - Give me a sec. I need to check my calendar.
        
    - Give me a sec. I’ll call you back.
        

---

### 句型 7：You’re always on me about...

- **含义：** 你总是在……这件事上催我/说我。
    
- **适合场景：** 家庭、朋友、工作压力。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** You’re always on me.
    
- **我的仿写：** You’re always on me about cleaning my room.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - My manager is always on me about deadlines.
        
    - My parents are always on me about saving money.
        
    - She’s always on me about being late.
        

---

### 句型 8：Can’t you just lay off for...?

- **含义：** 你就不能别烦我……吗？
    
- **适合场景：** 被催、被管、被批评时，语气比较直接。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** Can’t you just lay off for like one second?
    
- **我的仿写：** Can’t you just lay off for five minutes?
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Can you lay off for a bit?
        
    - Please lay off. I’m already stressed.
        
    - I wish people would lay off and let me think.
        

---

### 句型 9：I barely...

- **含义：** 我几乎没……
    
- **适合场景：** 描述程度很低、几乎没有做某事。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** I barely touched it.
    
- **我的仿写：** I barely slept last night.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - I barely know him.
        
    - I barely had time to eat.
        
    - I barely understood what he said.
        

---

### 句型 10：Don’t make a big deal about...

- **含义：** 别把……看得太严重；别小题大做。
    
- **适合场景：** 安慰别人、缓和气氛、表达事情没那么严重。
    
- **原文/接近原文：** We agreed not to make a big deal about this.
    
- **我的仿写：** Don’t make a big deal about it. It was just a mistake.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - I don’t want to make a big deal about it.
        
    - He made a big deal about a tiny problem.
        
    - It’s not a big deal. We can fix it.
