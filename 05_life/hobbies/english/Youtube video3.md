## 1. 中文总结

这段视频是一个英美早餐对比挑战。John Cena 和主持人先在纽约尝试美式早餐，包括煎饼、炒蛋、薯饼、培根、咖啡和甜甜圈。他们讨论了美式早餐“甜咸混合”“分量大”“热量高”的特点，也吐槽了美式滴滤咖啡。之后来到伦敦尝试英式早餐，包括香肠、培根、煎蛋、烤番茄、蘑菇、黑布丁、炸面包、豆子和 Marmite。John 一开始对豆子配吐司和 Marmite 有些怀疑，但试过后觉得英式早餐比看起来更丰富。最后他认为英式早餐不用加太多甜味，也能很好吃，所以把胜利给了英式早餐。

---

## 2. 分段英文整理版

### Part 1：The American breakfast challenge

**整理后的英文：**

Today, we’re putting American breakfast and English breakfast head to head. First up, American breakfast: pancakes, scrambled eggs, hash browns, bacon, and drip coffee. It looks like a lot, and honestly, it is a lot.

**中文解释：**

这一段开场介绍主题：比较美式早餐和英式早餐。美式早餐的特点是种类多、分量大，包括煎饼、炒蛋、薯饼、培根和咖啡。

---

### Part 2：A good pancake should stand alone

**整理后的英文：**

I asked for the syrup on the side because a good pancake should stand alone. You should try it plain first. If it needs syrup to taste good, maybe it’s not a great pancake.

**中文解释：**

John 说他把糖浆放在旁边，因为好的煎饼本身就应该好吃，不应该完全依赖糖浆。这是一个很实用的评价食物句型：**It should stand alone.**

---

### Part 3：Fixing bad coffee

**整理后的英文：**

Drip coffee isn’t great, so the American fix is to add a lot of cream. I used to get a huge cup of iced coffee and put in all kinds of flavored creamers. It was cheap, and it did the job.

**中文解释：**

他们吐槽美式滴滤咖啡不好喝，所以美国人的解决办法是加很多奶精或调味奶油。**It did the job** 表示“虽然不完美，但管用”。

---

### Part 4：Sweet, salty, and everything at once

**整理后的英文：**

You can stack the whole breakfast together: pancake, hash brown, eggs, bacon, and syrup on top. It’s sweet, salty, fatty, and savory all in one bite. I usually like to enjoy each thing separately, but somehow this works.

**中文解释：**

这一段讲美式早餐可以把各种东西叠在一起吃：甜的、咸的、油脂感和咸香味同时出现。**Somehow this works** 很地道，意思是“没想到这样还挺搭”。

---

### Part 5：Doughnuts for breakfast?

**整理后的英文：**

Doughnuts are a big part of American food culture, but in the UK they feel more like dessert or something you’d have in the afternoon. Still, once you start eating one, it’s hard to stop.

**中文解释：**

他们聊到甜甜圈是不是早餐。在美国，甜甜圈可以当早餐；但在英国，更像下午茶或甜点。**It’s hard to stop** 可以用来形容零食、游戏、刷短视频等。

---

### Part 6：John’s everyday breakfast

**整理后的英文：**

On a normal day, my go-to breakfast would be sourdough toast, poached eggs, and a flat white. If I’m feeling brave, I might have a second coffee.

**中文解释：**

John 说自己平时的早餐其实比较简单：酸面包、水波蛋和咖啡。**My go-to breakfast** 就是“我最常吃/最常选的早餐”。

---

### Part 7：Welcome to London

**整理后的英文：**

In London, we’re trying a proper English breakfast. There’s sausage, bacon, roasted tomato, mushrooms, fried egg, fried bread, baked beans, and black pudding. It’s definitely not a light breakfast.

**中文解释：**

来到伦敦后，他们开始尝试传统英式早餐。英式早餐也很丰盛，并不清淡。这里的 **proper English breakfast** 表示“正宗的英式早餐”。

---

### Part 8：Trying black pudding

**整理后的英文：**

Black pudding has a very distinct taste. If you’re not used to it, I can understand why you might not like it. But I’m okay with it. It’s different, but not terrible.

**中文解释：**

John 尝试黑布丁，觉得味道很特别。如果没吃惯，可能会不喜欢，但他觉得还能接受。**I’m okay with it** 是非常实用的口语表达，意思是“我可以接受”。

---

### Part 9：Beans on toast

**整理后的英文：**

At first, beans on toast sounded too plain to me. But after trying it, I have to say it’s better than it looks. The sauce is a little sweet, and the bread soaks it up nicely.

**中文解释：**

John 一开始觉得豆子配吐司太普通，但试过后觉得比看起来好吃。**It’s better than it looks** 是评价食物、衣服、房间、产品时都能用的句子。

---

### Part 10：Marmite divides people

**整理后的英文：**

Marmite is one of those things you either love or hate. It has a really strong taste, and for me, it’s not something I’d choose again. I’m glad I tried it, but it’s not my thing.

**中文解释：**

Marmite 是英国一种酵母酱，味道很重，很多人要么很喜欢，要么很讨厌。**It’s not my thing** 是很自然的表达，表示“这不是我的菜”。

---

### Part 11：The final decision

**整理后的英文：**

In the end, I’d give the points to the English breakfast. The American breakfast is good, but it relies a lot on sugar. The English breakfast is mostly savory, and it still stands on its own.

**中文解释：**

最后 John 认为英式早餐赢了，因为美式早餐比较依赖糖，而英式早餐主要是咸口，却依然很好吃。**I’d give the points to...** 可以用来表达“我会把分数给……”。

## 5. 句型模板

### 句型 1：Sounds like...

- **含义：** 听起来像……
    
- **适合场景：** 回应别人的建议、计划、挑战
    
- **原文/接近原文：** Sounds like a challenge.
    
- **我的仿写：** Sounds like a good plan.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Sounds like a lot of work.
        
    - Sounds like a fun weekend.
        
    - Sounds like something I’d enjoy.
        

---

### 句型 2：I asked for... on the side.

- **含义：** 我要求把……另外放
    
- **适合场景：** 点餐、咖啡店、餐厅
    
- **原文/接近原文：** I asked for the syrup on the side.
    
- **我的仿写：** I asked for the sauce on the side.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Can I have the dressing on the side?
        
    - I asked for milk on the side.
        
    - Could you put the chili sauce on the side?
        

---

### 句型 3：A good... should stand alone.

- **含义：** 好的……本身就应该够好
    
- **适合场景：** 评价食物、作品、产品、能力
    
- **原文/接近原文：** A good pancake should stand alone.
    
- **我的仿写：** A good coffee should stand alone.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - A good product should stand alone.
        
    - A good story should stand alone.
        
    - A good presentation should stand alone.
        

---

### 句型 4：I’m not a big fan of...

- **含义：** 我不太喜欢……
    
- **适合场景：** 委婉表达不喜欢
    
- **原文/接近原文：** I’m not a big fan of that.
    
- **我的仿写：** I’m not a big fan of crowded places.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - I’m not a big fan of sweet coffee.
        
    - I’m not a big fan of waking up early.
        
    - I’m not a big fan of very formal restaurants.
        

---

### 句型 5：It would benefit from...

- **含义：** 如果加上……会更好
    
- **适合场景：** 提建议、评价食物/工作成果
    
- **原文/接近原文：** It would benefit from some butter.
    
- **我的仿写：** This dish would benefit from more sauce.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - This article would benefit from a clearer structure.
        
    - The room would benefit from better lighting.
        
    - The presentation would benefit from more examples.
        

---

### 句型 6：Somehow, this works.

- **含义：** 不知道为什么，但这样还挺行
    
- **适合场景：** 意外发现某事效果不错
    
- **原文/接近原文：** Somehow, this works.
    
- **我的仿写：** Somehow, this combination works.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Somehow, this outfit works.
        
    - Somehow, this weird schedule works for me.
        
    - Somehow, this simple method works really well.
        

---

### 句型 7：My go-to is...

- **含义：** 我最常选的是……
    
- **适合场景：** 聊饮食、习惯、工具、娱乐
    
- **原文/接近原文：** My go-to is a poached egg on toast.
    
- **我的仿写：** My go-to is an iced Americano.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - My go-to breakfast is oatmeal and eggs.
        
    - My go-to app is Notion.
        
    - My go-to weekend activity is going to the gym.
        

---

### 句型 8：If you’re not used to it, I can understand why...

- **含义：** 如果你不习惯，我能理解为什么……
    
- **适合场景：** 解释文化差异、饮食差异、生活习惯
    
- **原文/接近原文：** If you’re not used to it, I can understand why you’d be like, “Nah.”
    
- **我的仿写：** If you’re not used to it, I can understand why it feels strange.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - If you’re not used to spicy food, I can understand why it feels too much.
        
    - If you’re not used to working late, I can understand why it’s tiring.
        
    - If you’re not used to British humor, I can understand why it feels confusing.
        

---

### 句型 9：It’s better than it looks.

- **含义：** 它比看起来好
    
- **适合场景：** 评价食物、酒店、产品、地方
    
- **原文/接近原文：** It’s better than it looks.
    
- **我的仿写：** This restaurant is better than it looks.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - The hotel is better than it looks online.
        
    - This dish is better than it looks.
        
    - The movie is better than it looks from the trailer.
        

---

### 句型 10：It’s not my thing.

- **含义：** 这不是我的菜
    
- **适合场景：** 礼貌拒绝、不喜欢某事
    
- **原文/接近原文：** It’s not my thing.
    
- **我的仿写：** Marmite is not really my thing.
    
- **更多例句：**
    
    - Camping is not really my thing.
        
    - Loud bars are not my thing.
        
    - Horror movies are not my thing.
